Privacy and limitations

OCR is powered by Tesseract.js, loaded from a public CDN. On first use it downloads software and English language data. Recognition then runs on your phone, without uploading video frames. Internet is needed for initial OCR setup and may be needed again if your browser clears its cache. The page does not store camera frames. Printed text works best; handwriting is experimental and may be inaccurate. This checks arithmetic equations, not algebra. A secure HTTPS website or localhost is required for camera access.
