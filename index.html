Important limitations

Printed equations work best. Handwriting, long division, carries/borrows, column alignment, and crowded worksheets may be misread. Vertical division is supported as “÷” or “/” in a stacked three-line format, not traditional long-division brackets. Always confirm recognized numbers before relying on a result. Scanning a full worksheet is slower and less reliable than scanning a few well-spaced problems. The page loads Tesseract.js and English OCR data from external CDNs initially; image frames remain on the device. Offline use after setup is not guaranteed.
